Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the hotel?

Hotel Middle Star is on Gantavya Street, Lakeside-6, Pokhara, a true lakefront hotel with Phewa Lake just a few steps away.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Travel sites list check-in from 12:00 and check-out by 10:00. Please call us to confirm, especially for early arrivals or late departures.

Is Wi-Fi free?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available for guests.

What comes with the rooms?

Rooms have air conditioning, large comfortable beds, wooden parquet floors, a working desk and an attached bathroom with hot and cold shower.

Is there a restaurant?

Yes, our roof top restaurant and bar serves food and drinks with a wide view of Phewa Lake and the hills.

How can I book a room?

Reserve through Booking.com, Agoda, Trip.com, Tripadvisor or KAYAK from the Rooms page, or call us on 061-456468, 9856038990 or 9856038991, or use the contact form above.
